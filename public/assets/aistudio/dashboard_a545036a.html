Set up nutrient monitoring

Add a soil EC/TDS probe to get an alert when nutrient levels fall too low.

  1. Connect the probeAnalog EC/TDS probe to pin 32 on the controller.
  2. Turn on monitoringPress the button below.
  3. Set the alert level400 ppm is a sensible start. Adjust for your crop.

Nutrient trend

Readings count only while soil moisture is at least 30%.

What to do when nutrients are low
  1. Apply fertiliserUse a balanced NPK fertiliser at the label rate for your crop.
  2. Irrigate to dissolve itWater moves the fertiliser to the root zone.
  3. Recheck after an hourThe reading rises as it dissolves. You'll get a message when it's back to normal.

Phone alerts

Receive alerts on your phone even when this app is closed.

  1. Install the free ntfy appAndroid and iPhone.
  2. Subscribe to a private topicChoose a name nobody can guess, for example smartagri-k7x29qd.
  3. Add the same topic to the controllerSet NTFY_TOPIC in the sketch and upload it again.
